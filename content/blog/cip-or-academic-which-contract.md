Before you sign anything, find out which of two contracts your placement uses. It is the single fact that decides whether you are paid.

## Academic internship (convention de stage)

- **Who:** students still enrolled in a programme.
- **Who signs:** you, your university, and the employer (three parties).
- **Pay:** generally **unpaid**; only real expenses are reimbursed.
- **Point:** learning, tied to your curriculum, with a training plan.

## Professional Immersion Agreement (CIP / BIO)

- **Who:** graduates and jobseekers no longer enrolled.
- **Who signs:** you and the employer, under a regulated framework.
- **Pay:** a **legally indexed minimum — about €1,095 gross/month full-time (2026)**.
- **Point:** a genuine step into work, with a written training plan, overseen by Bruxelles Formation (FR) or VDAB (NL).

## Why it matters

The two look similar from the outside — same desk, same tasks — but only one guarantees a stipend. A recurring problem in Brussels is graduates being placed on the *unpaid academic* track when they should be on the *paid CIP* track. If you have already graduated, that mismatch is worth challenging.

## One question to ask

> "Is this a CIP placement with the indexed stipend, or an unpaid academic convention?"

If the answer is vague, that is itself useful information. Read more on the [Know your rights](/rights.html) page.
